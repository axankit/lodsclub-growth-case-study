# 09 · Meta campaign — LC_Anime_Sales_Nov26

**Goal:** first paying customers at ≤ ₹250 each. **Budget:** ₹3,000 over 7 days (13 → 20 Nov 2026).

## Structure
| Ad set | Lifetime budget | Audience | Ads | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AS1 New buyers | ₹2,300 | India, 18–30 as a suggestion, Advantage+ audience | New 15 s reel · proven reel (CTR 1.26%, CPC ₹1.79) · C05 carousel | `/anime/` landing |
| AS2 Warm people | ₹700 | Cart/checkout no-buy 30d + site visitors 30d + IG engagers 60d | C06 Top-5 carousel · reel with "still thinking?" copy | Product pages / landing |

## Key decisions
- **Optimise for Purchase**, not checkout — the previous campaign optimised for checkouts and got checkouts.
- **Lifetime budgets per ad set** with a fixed end date → even pacing, hard spend cap.
- **Few ads** — ₹430/day split across many ads means none of them learn.
- **Old campaign paused** before the spend limit was lifted, so it couldn't eat the new budget.
- **UTM on every link** for creative-level attribution in the Visitor Log.

## Kill / scale rules
- Day 1–2: no edits (learning).
- Day 3: pause any ad with ₹200 spent and 0 add-to-carts.
- Day 5: move remaining budget to the best ad.
