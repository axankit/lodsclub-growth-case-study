From cheap clicks to paying customers.

Meta performance marketing and conversion optimisation for LodsClub, an Indian D2C brand selling metal-back anime phone cases at ₹249. The ads worked. The website didn't. So I rebuilt the path from ad click to UPI payment, then relaunched the ads into it.

Role: growth marketer — ads, website, tracking, creative · Stack: Meta Ads, WooCommerce, Elementor, Google Sheets + Apps Script, Playwright, ffmpeg

01 · DIAGNOSISThe ads were fine. The funnel leaked.

The best reel delivered 182k impressions at a 1.26% CTR. People clicked, browsed and added to cart — and then almost nobody paid.

What I found

  • Surprise shipping: ₹60 revealed only after the full address. With it, 1 of 31 orders got paid; with free delivery, 3 of 7.
  • Cache bug: some mobile visitors from ads were served the homepage instead of the page the ad promised. I wrote a plain-language handover for the hosting team; they fixed it.
  • Product page: price below the fold, phone picker buried, 126 Samsung models in one dropdown, one photo, "Reviews (0)".
  • Speed: 5–7 MB pages, ~185 requests.
  • No visibility: no way to see which step lost people.

Decision: fix the funnel first, relaunch ads second. Cash on delivery was ruled out by the client, so every change had to make prepaid UPI feel safe and simple.

02 · CHECKOUT & OFFERKill the surprise.

  • Free delivery until the first 20 new orders, shown before checkout everywhere (ticker, product page, landing).
  • UPI-first wording at payment; phone number made required for couriers.
  • MRP made consistent (₹500 → ₹499 on 40 cases) so every case reads "₹249 · ₹499 · 50% OFF".

03 · LANDING PAGE /ANIME/A page that continues the ad.

Ads used to drop people on a generic category page sorted A–Z. I built a dedicated anime landing page — dark stage, speed lines, Japanese titles, every series with real product cut-outs, Google reviews and an FAQ.

Then I made it 3× faster

WordPress was loading the whole theme (a 1.1 MB stylesheet, sliders, icon packs) on a page that used none of it. A small PHP rule now strips those files on this page only, keeping the pixel, analytics and WhatsApp, lazy-loading lower images and preloading the hero.

07 · TRACKINGSee every step.

The pixel tells you what happened, not where people gave up. A lightweight script sends one row per event to a Google Sheet:

Page viewProduct viewPhone selectedAdd to cartCheckoutPlace orderPayment pageWhatsApp click

With page type, scroll depth, time on page and UTM source, internal traffic excluded. Every new ad link carries utm_content = ad name, so each visitor maps to the exact creative.

09 · META CAMPAIGNOptimise for the thing you want.

The previous campaign optimised for checkouts — and got checkouts. The relaunch optimises for Purchase, on ₹3,000 over 7 days.
